# Smart Attendance Monitoring & Analytics System (SAMS)

SAMS is a smart web-based attendance management system designed for colleges and educational institutions. It helps faculty and administrators manage attendance efficiently while allowing students to view their attendance records and analytics.

## 🚀 Live Demo

[Smart Attendance – Institutional Attendance & Analytics System](https://smart-attendance-8803.ai.studio/)

## 📌 Problem Statement

Traditional attendance management is often time-consuming and can lead to errors, manual work, and difficulty in monitoring student attendance.

SAMS provides a centralized digital platform to simplify attendance recording, monitoring, and analysis.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

SAMS provides an easy-to-use platform where attendance can be managed digitally and attendance information can be analyzed through dashboards and reports.

The system helps:

- Reduce manual attendance work
- Maintain centralized attendance records
- Monitor student attendance
- Identify low-attendance students
- Provide useful attendance analytics
- Improve transparency for students and faculty

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍🏫 Faculty
- Manage attendance
- Record student attendance
- Monitor attendance records
- View attendance information and analytics
- Manage classes/subjects

### 👨‍🎓 Students
- View attendance records
- Check attendance percentage
- Monitor subject-wise attendance
- Track their attendance status

### 👨‍💼 Administration
- Manage students and faculty
- Monitor institutional attendance
- View attendance analytics
- Access attendance reports
